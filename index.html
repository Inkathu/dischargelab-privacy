Privacy Policy - Discharge Lab

Last updated: 2026-09-15

Discharge Lab is a controlled battery-discharge tool for Android. This page explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your data. There is not much to explain, because the app was built not to need your data in the first place.

The short version

Discharge Lab does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no access to the internet at all - it cannot send anything anywhere, because it has no way to connect to a network.

What the app reads from your device

While the app is open, it reads battery telemetry that Android already exposes to any app: charge level, temperature, voltage, current, and charging status, along with the system's thermal state and the frequencies of the CPU cores. It uses these readings for two things only: showing them on screen as charts and a table, and deciding when to pause or stop the session (for example, when the temperature limit is reached).

Where that data goes

Nowhere but your screen, and nowhere but your device. This telemetry lives only in the app's memory while it is running. It is not written to a server, because the app has no network access to reach one. The app never sends it anywhere by itself. Nothing leaves the phone unless you export or share it yourself. When you close the app, that in-memory history is gone unless you explicitly exported it first.

The CSV export

Discharge Lab can save a session's telemetry history as a CSV file, together with a short text file that explains its columns and units. This happens only when you tap the export action yourself - never automatically, never in the background. When you do, Android's own folder picker opens and you choose the folder; the app writes those two files there and nowhere else. From that point on, they are normal files on your device, under your control like any other document you save. The About screen also hides a diagnostics report, behind ten taps on the version number. It lists the phone model, the Android version and the current battery and sensor readings, and it opens Android's share sheet so you can send it if something looks wrong. It holds no personal data and is never sent on its own.

Permissions

The only permission Discharge Lab requests is vibration, used solely for an optional vibration-load test mode you can leave turned off entirely. The app does not request or use internet, camera, location, contacts, microphone, or storage-wide access.

Children

Discharge Lab does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, of any age, for the reasons above - there is no collection mechanism to begin with.
